## The AI Information System of tomorrow

Enterprises built their IT by accumulation, dozens of disconnected tools, each its own silo. Generative AI is repeating that mistake faster, scattering ungoverned assistants across the organization.

Prisme.ai is the **unified foundation** that stops it: one secure, cloud-agnostic platform where technical and business teams build, govern, and run AI together, agents, full-stack applications, and data, with shared traceability, security, and governance. From a first chat experiment to production multi-agent systems, without another silo.
