## The AI Information System of tomorrow

The software your business needs is increasingly **generated by LLMs**. The technology is no longer the subject. The subject is shipping the **right digital experience for each channel and use case**: a web app, a chatbot, an agent inside Teams, or a workflow behind the scenes, without turning your stack into chaos.

The default path is a patchwork: one tool for the front, another for the chatbot, another for the workflow, and endless glue between them. That is how IT became a pile of silos, and generative AI is repeating it faster.

Prisme.ai is the **one stack** that replaces the patchwork. Build the front-end, the agents, and the workflows in the same place, connected by default. The real value is not "AI agents", it is **resilience across clouds and models, security, traceability, and ROI**: you turn the shift into a clean Information System at the service of the business. IT as a service.
