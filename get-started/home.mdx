## Getting Started Paths

Whatever you want to ship, a chat assistant, an agent inside your tools, a web app, or a workflow, it runs on the same foundation. Pick the entry point that matches how you work; most teams use both.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Business teams (no-code)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Set up your account">
        Complete your [account setup](./account-setup) to personalize your experience.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start in Chat">
        Give everyone secure, multi-LLM [Chat](../products/ai-securechat/overview) for day-to-day work.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Ground it on your knowledge">
        Turn your documents and sites into knowledge your agents can use with [Knowledges](/products/ai-knowledge/overview).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Build what you need">
        An agent, an assistant, or a simple app: build it in [Agent Creator](/products/agent-factory/creating-agents), then publish it in Chat, embed it in Teams, or put it on your site. Browse [use cases](/resources/use-cases/overview) for ideas.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Measure impact">
        Track adoption and quality with [Insights](/products/ai-insights/overview).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technical teams (full-code)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Understand the platform">
        Review the [platform overview](./platform-overview): maturity, resilience, and governance.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set up your account">
        Complete your [account setup](./account-setup) with API access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Build in Builder">
        Build React front-ends, agentic workflows, and endpoints in [Builder](../products/ai-builder/overview), versioned with Git. A web app, a chatbot, an agent, or all three.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Integrate everywhere">
        Use the [API, MCP, and connectors](/api-reference/introduction) to embed agents and apps in Teams, WhatsApp, or your own site.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Govern at scale">
        Configure multi-tenancy, roles, and model access in [Governe](/products/ai-governance/overview).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
