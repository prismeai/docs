## Getting Started Paths

Two entry points onto the same foundation. Pick the one that matches how you work, most teams end up using both.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Business teams (no-code)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Set up your account">
        Complete your [account setup](./account-setup) to personalize your experience.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Explore Chat">
        Start with [Chat](../products/ai-securechat/overview) to experience secure conversational AI.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Manage a knowledge base">
        Turn your documents into agent-ready knowledge with [Knowledges](/products/ai-knowledge/overview).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Build and share your first agent">
        Create a [specialized agent](/products/agent-factory/creating-agents) and publish it to your team.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Measure impact">
        Track adoption and quality with [Insights](/products/ai-insights/overview).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technical teams (full-code)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Understand the architecture">
        Review the [platform overview](./platform-overview), architecture, lifecycle, and governance.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set up your account">
        Complete your [account setup](./account-setup) with API access.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Build in Builder">
        Develop full-stack apps and agentic workflows in [Builder](../products/ai-builder/overview), versioned with Git.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Integrate everywhere">
        Use the [API and MCP](/api-reference/introduction) to embed and consume capabilities elsewhere.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Govern at scale">
        Configure multi-tenancy, roles, and model access in [Governe](/products/ai-governance/overview).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
