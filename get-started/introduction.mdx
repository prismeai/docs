## The problem: your IT already lives in silos. AI is making it worse.

For the last twenty years, every new business need has meant a new piece of software. Layer after layer, enterprises built their information systems by accumulation: dozens of disconnected tools, each with its own data, its own logs, its own access model. The result is a landscape that nobody can fully govern, secure, or reason about end to end.

Generative AI is now repeating that same mistake, faster. Teams spin up assistants and copilots everywhere, on different stacks, with no shared traceability, no unified access control, and no way to measure what any of it actually delivers. You don't get an AI strategy. You get **another generation of silos**, this time probabilistic and even harder to audit.

## The shift: the AI Information System of tomorrow

**Prisme.ai is the unified foundation where your agents, your full-stack applications, and your data live together**, instead of scattering a new silo for every use case.

Think of it as the **Information System for AI**: a clean, purpose-built architecture where **technical and business teams collaborate on the same platform** to deliver value, in a way that is **secure, traceable, and scalable** by default. Business teams, developers, and IT govern the *same* system, with the *same* controls, from a first chat experiment all the way to production applications serving thousands of users.

Because it's one foundation, you **pool what used to be duplicated in every silo**: infrastructure, security, compliance, and data protection are solved once, for everything built on top.

### Full-stack, agentic development

On Prisme.ai you build complete applications: a front-end interface in React, and a back-end composed of **agentic workflows**. A traditional workflow is rigid and perfectly reproducible; an agentic workflow can call an agent, introducing a probabilistic, adaptive dimension exactly where your business logic needs judgment rather than a fixed rule. Both live on the same platform, so an agent can be embedded inside a web flow, and a web flow can hand off to an agent.

### Governed by design

Because everything runs on one foundation, governance is native rather than bolted on. And it works on **two levels at once**: a **central governance** sets the rules for the whole platform, while **each organization inherits from it** and adapts within those guardrails. That balance is the point: teams get the **freedom and agility** to move fast, while the enterprise keeps **compliance** guaranteed end to end.

The platform handles the cross-cutting concerns *once*, for everyone:

- **Multi-tenancy** cleanly separates organizations, teams, and environments, each inheriting central policy.
- **SSO and centralized permissions**: one identity model, one place to grant and revoke access.
- **Audit logs**: every action is recorded and traceable for security and compliance reviews.
- **Chargeback / rebilling**: usage is attributed per organization, so costs are transparent and allocatable.
- **Every agent has a service account** with its own roles: restrict which LLMs it may use or which tools it may call, from the organization level down.

### Traceable natively

Prisme.ai is **event-driven**: processing is asynchronous, scalable, and every operation emits a **normalized event**: consistent types, correlation IDs, and timestamps. Those events are historized and aggregated, which is what makes observability, analytics, security monitoring, and debugging (follow a single **correlation ID** across every API call and tool execution) possible out of the box.

## Built to live everywhere

A foundation is only useful if it reaches your users where they already are. Prisme.ai is **API-first**, and every capability is exposed through **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**, so your agents and applications can be embedded in your websites, portals, and existing tools, or consumed from other systems entirely. Ready-made **connectors** push the same agents out to the channels your teams already use: **Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, WhatsApp, Facebook**, without rebuilding them. The platform doesn't lock value inside its own UI; it lets that value flow wherever your business needs it.

And because the foundation owns the plumbing, it gives you **resilience where it matters most**:

Prisme.ai exists to remove the frustration of digital complexity, so your teams spend their energy **solving business problems, not managing the technical stack**. The plumbing (scalability, security, traceability, governance) is handled by the foundation; the value is what you build on top of it.
