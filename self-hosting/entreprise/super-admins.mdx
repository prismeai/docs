---
title: 'Configure platform administrators'
description: 'Platform administrator: grant the superadmin platform role through SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS or Helm, and the org-creator role from Governance, on a self-hosted Prisme.ai deployment.'
---

Use this procedure to decide who administers a self-hosted platform as a whole. When it succeeds, the accounts you list sign in as `superadmin` and reach every organization and every workspace.

A platform role sits above organization and workspace roles. For how it combines with them, see [How access control works](/products/access-control/overview).

| Platform role | Grants | How it is assigned |
|---|---|---|
| `superadmin` | Full access to every organization and every workspace, the **Platform** section of Governance, and product installation | `SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS`, or the `platformRole` field of the user |
| `root` | Full access to every organization, but no access to workspaces, pages, or events | The `platformRole` field of the user |
| `org-creator` | Creating organizations, up to `ORGS_MAX_PER_CREATOR` per person | **Platform**, **Users** in Governance, or the `platformRole` field |

A user holds at most one platform role.

## Before you begin

- **Who:** the operator of the deployment, with access to the API gateway configuration or to the Helm values of the `prismeai-core` chart.
- **Requires:** the email addresses of the accounts to promote. They sign in with these addresses, through a local account or an SSO provider.
- **Impact:** a `superadmin` can read and change everything on the platform, including every organization's members, roles, and workspaces.

## Grant the superadmin role

Set the list of email addresses, separated by commas, in one of these places:

- **Helm:** in the `prismeai-core` chart values.

  ```yaml
  prismeai-api-gateway:
    config:
      admins: 'admin1@example.com,admin2@example.com'
  ```

- **Environment variable:** on the `api-gateway` service.

  ```bash
  SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS=admin1@example.com,admin2@example.com
  ```

Apply the change and restart the API gateway. The listed accounts hold `superadmin` from their next request.

## Grant the right to create organizations

1. Sign in as a `superadmin`, open Governance, and select **Platform**, then **Users**.
2. In the user's row, open the actions menu and select **Allow to create organizations**. If the user already holds another platform role, confirm with **Replace the role**.

The user's **Platform Role** column shows **Organization creator**. To create more organizations than `ORGS_MAX_PER_CREATOR` allows, or to let every user create organizations, see [Who can create an organization](/products/ai-governance/identity-access#who-can-create-an-organization).

## Verify the result

1. Sign in with one of the listed accounts.
2. Open Governance and confirm that the **Platform** section appears, listing every organization.
3. Open a Builder workspace you are not shared on, and confirm it loads.

## Recommendations

- Keep the list short, and use these accounts for installation, upgrades, and platform configuration.
- For day-to-day administration of an organization, give people the **Owner** or **Admin** role in that organization instead. See [Create and assign organization roles](/products/access-control/manage-organization-roles).
- Review the list at each upgrade, and remove accounts of people who left.

## Undo the change

- **superadmin from configuration:** remove the address from `config.admins` or `SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS`, apply, and restart the API gateway.
- **Organization creator:** in **Platform**, **Users**, select **Revoke organization creation** in the user's row.
