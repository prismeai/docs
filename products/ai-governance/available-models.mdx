Access to AI models is one of the cross-cutting concerns Governe handles once for the whole platform, so every agent draws from the same governed catalog. This page lists the models enabled by default on the Prisme.ai platform, grouped by provider. Use it as a reference when configuring agents, RAG pipelines, or routing rules.

<Note>
The catalog below reflects the **default platform configuration**, as served in production on 7 September 2026. Available models in your environment may differ depending on:
- Provider credentials configured by your platform administrator.
- Organization-level restrictions set in [Model Governance](/products/ai-governance/model-governance).
- Per-agent model restrictions.

Some models are also published to a **single organization**, and appear only for its members. To check what is actually allowed for your organization, go to **Agents Controls > Models** in AI Governance.
</Note>

## Cost tiers

Each completion model is tagged with a cost tier used by routing strategies (e.g. *Cost Optimized*) and by the soft-downgrade policy:

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| <span style={{color: '#10b981', fontWeight: 600}}>low</span> | Cheapest tier: small / fast models, suited for high-volume tasks. |
| <span style={{color: '#f59e0b', fontWeight: 600}}>medium</span> | Balanced cost / quality: default for most general-purpose agents. |
| <span style={{color: '#ef4444', fontWeight: 600}}>high</span> | Most capable / most expensive: reserved for complex reasoning, long contexts, or final-quality outputs. |
| - | Not applicable (embedding and image generation models). |
