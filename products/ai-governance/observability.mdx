Because the platform is natively traceable, every request already carries a correlation ID through an event-driven backbone, and Observability is where that raw event stream becomes org-wide insight. Open it from **Observability** in the Governance sidebar. It has two tabs: **Platform** for organization-wide operations, and **LLM** for LLM Gateway cost and usage. Pick a time range (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d) from the top of the page; it applies to both tabs.

<Note>
Observability here is scoped to your organization as a whole. The per-workspace and per-automation deep-dive (metrics, latency, error detail, usage, dependencies, and traces) now lives in the **Builder**, per workspace, under **Builder > workspace > Observability**. See [Builder Observability](/products/ai-builder/observability). Rows in the Platform tab link straight through to the matching workspace's Observability in Builder.
</Note>

## Platform tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-governance-observability-platform.png" alt="Platform operations tab" />
</Frame>

The Platform tab is an operations view across every workspace in your organization. It focuses on health and errors, not adoption; usage and adoption metrics live in AI Insights, and a link points there instead of duplicating them.

- **Quick stats**: Unique Users, Total Interactions, Error Rate, and Average RPM across the org for the selected period. The error rate turns red once it crosses the alert threshold.
- **Top workspaces by errors**: workspaces ranked by error count, each row showing interactions, users, error count, and error rate. Click a row to open that workspace's Observability in Builder.
- **Recent errors**: the latest error events with their code, message, timestamp, and originating workspace. Each links through to the source workspace in Builder.

<Info>
Some blocks render progressively: they stay empty until the org has activity in the selected period.
</Info>

## LLM tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-governance-observability-llm.png" alt="LLM cost and usage tab" />
</Frame>

The LLM tab is the LLM Gateway cost and usage view, sourced from the `analytics.llm.completion` events emitted by the **llm-gateway** workspace.

- **Headline cards**: Total Cost, Input Tokens, Output Tokens, LLM Calls, and Carbon (kg CO₂eq).
- **Cost by Model**: per-model breakdown with cost share, token count, and call count.
- **Cost by Provider**: call count and average latency per provider.
- **Cost Timeline**: per-bucket cost trend across the selected period.

This is the right place to track LLM spend org-wide. For a single workspace's cost and performance, use that workspace's Observability in Builder.

## Where per-workspace detail lives

The deep-dive that used to live here, health scores, latency percentiles, error tables with activity-feed deep links, usage timelines, dependency graphs, and the real-time event feed, now lives in the **Builder**, scoped to one workspace at a time.

To investigate a specific workspace or automation:

1. From the **Platform** tab, click the workspace row (or open the workspace directly in Builder).
2. Open **Observability** on that workspace in Builder.
3. Use its per-workspace tabs to drill into metrics, latency, errors, usage, dependencies, and traces.

See [Builder Observability](/products/ai-builder/observability) for the full per-workspace reference.

<Warning>
**Access to raw events stays bound to each workspace's ACLs.** Governance surfaces aggregated, org-wide metrics (error counts, cost, RPM) suitable for a platform, security, or FinOps owner. Drilling from a metric down to the underlying events in Builder requires access to that workspace, either through an explicit share or platform-wide superadmin rights. The metrics travel up into the Governance scope; the underlying data stays compartmentalized below it.
</Warning>
