title: 'Control access to a workspace'
description: 'Workspace owner: share a Builder workspace with the owner, editor, or custom roles, and write the security rules that define those roles and what anonymous visitors can do.'
---

Use this procedure to decide who can work on a Builder workspace, who can use the pages and apps it serves, and what each of them can do. When it succeeds, each person you share the workspace with holds a role, and the workspace security rules grant that role exactly the actions you declared.

Workspace access is independent of the organization role. For how the two relate, see [How access control works](/products/access-control/overview).

## Before you begin

- **Who:** an owner of the workspace. Editors cannot share the workspace or change its security rules.
- **Where:** Builder, in the workspace, **Settings**, tabs **Sharing** and **Security**.
- **Requires:** the email address of each person to add. They must belong to your organization and have signed in to the platform at least once.
- **Impact:** security rules apply to every request on the workspace as soon as you save them, including requests from end users of its pages and apps.

## Built-in roles

Every workspace has two built-in roles. They cannot be removed.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Everything on the workspace: edit and delete it, share it, edit its security rules and API keys, read all its events, run and test its automations, and manage its pages, apps, files, and secure secrets. The person who creates the workspace is its first owner. |
| `editor` | Read and update the workspace, its pages, and its installed apps, manage its files, read its events, and run and test its automations. An editor cannot delete the workspace, share it, or change its security rules. |

Any other role is a custom role that the workspace declares in its security rules.

## Share the workspace

1. In Builder, open the workspace and select **Settings**, then the **Sharing** tab.
2. In **Invite a member**, enter the person's email address.
3. In **Select role**, choose `owner`, `editor`, or a custom role.
4. Select **Add**. The person appears under **Members** with their role.

To change a member's role later, choose another role in their row. To revoke access, select **Remove member** in their row.

## Write the security rules

The security rules are a YAML document with an `authorizations` key holding two lists: `roles`, the roles the workspace declares, and `rules`, what each role may do. For the complete syntax, see [Workspace security rules reference](/products/access-control/workspace-rules-reference).

<Warning>
A rule without `role` applies to everyone, including you and anonymous visitors, and rules you write take precedence over the built-in `owner` and `editor` grants. A deny rule (`inverted: true`) without `role` can therefore lock every owner out of the workspace. Only a platform `superadmin` can then restore access.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rules">
    In Builder, open the workspace and select **Settings**, then the **Security** tab. The **Security Rules** editor shows the current YAML.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare the roles">
    Under `authorizations.roles`, add one key per custom role. Also declare `editor: {}` if a rule extends or restricts the built-in editor.

    ```yaml
    authorizations:
      roles:
        editor: {}
        reader:
          description: Reads the pages labelled "internal"
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant actions to each role">
    Under `authorizations.rules`, add one rule per grant. A rule names a `role`, one or more `action`, one or more `subject`, and optional `conditions`.

    ```yaml
      rules:
        - role: reader
          action: read
          subject: pages
          conditions:
            labels:
              $in:
                - internal
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: assign a role automatically">
    To give a role to every user who signs in through a given identity provider, add an `auth` block to the role. The key is the provider name: `prismeai` for Prisme.ai accounts, or the name of a configured SSO provider. Conditions filter on the user's authentication data.

    ```yaml
      roles:
        reader:
          auth:
            yourOwnSso: {}
            prismeai:
              conditions:
                authData.email:
                  $regex: ^.*@example\.com$
    ```

    With this block, every user signed in through `yourOwnSso`, and every Prisme.ai account with an `example.com` email address, holds `reader` without being added in **Sharing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rules">
    Select **Save Rules**. When a rule is invalid, the editor shows the error and the previous roles and rules stay in effect: for example, `Unknown custom role 'reader' referenced in a rule` when a rule names a role that `roles` does not declare. Fix the YAML and save again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the result

1. Sign in as a member who holds the custom role, or ask one to.
2. Open a resource the role may access, such as a page labelled `internal`, and confirm that it loads.
3. Open a resource the role may not access, and confirm that it is refused with an HTTP `403` response or an access denied message.

## Common patterns

- To let end users open a Builder app and call only some of its endpoints, see [Role-based access for your app](/products/ai-builder/spa-access-control).
- To restrict who can trigger an automation, see [Restrict who can run an automation](/products/access-control/protect-automations).
- To give a script or an automation API access to the workspace, see [Create workspace API keys](/products/access-control/workspace-api-keys).
- To let members store their own third-party credentials, see [Let members deposit their own credentials](/products/access-control/secure-secrets).

## Undo the change

- Before saving, select **Reset** to discard your edits in the **Security Rules** editor.
- After saving, edit the YAML back and select **Save Rules** again. If the workspace is versioned, you can also restore the `security.yml` of an earlier version. See [Versioning](/products/ai-builder/versioning).
- A custom role that is still assigned to a member cannot be removed from `roles`: saving fails with `Can't delete <role> role as it is still assigned to some user in your workspace`. Change those members' role in **Sharing** first.
