---
title: 'Create and assign organization roles'
description: 'Organization administrator: create a custom role in Governance, restrict it to specific agents, knowledge bases, or models, and assign it to members.'
---

Use this procedure to give a group of members exactly the access they need in your organization, by creating a custom role and assigning it. When it succeeds, the members you assigned see only the products and resources the role allows.

## Before you begin

- **Who:** to create or edit a role, the **Owner** of the organization, or a member whose role holds `orgs:roles:manage`. The **Admin** role can read roles but not change them. To assign a role, a member whose role holds `orgs:members:manage`, such as **Admin**.
- **Where:** Governance, in the organization the role belongs to.
- **Requires:** the list of permissions the role needs. See [Organization roles and permissions](/products/access-control/organization-roles). For a role restricted to specific resources, the IDs of those agents, knowledge bases, or models.
- **Impact:** a role change applies to every member holding the role, in this organization only. Other organizations are not affected.

<Tip>
Start from the closest system role. Select **Duplicate role** on it, then remove what the new role does not need. This is safer than editing the system role, which would change it for every member who holds it.
</Tip>

## Create a role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the roles list">
    In Governance, select **Roles**. The list shows system roles and the organization's custom roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the role">
    Select **Create Role**, or select **Duplicate role** on an existing role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role">
    Enter a **Slug**, such as `data-analyst`, and a **Name**, such as `Data Analyst`. The slug cannot be changed later. Optional: enter a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the permissions">
    Under **Platform Permissions**, check the actions the role needs. Checking a product or a resource grants all of its actions through a wildcard, such as `storage:*` or `storage:vector_stores:*`.

    Optional: under **Custom Permissions**, add permissions that the tree does not list, in the `scope:action` format, then select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the role">
    Select **Create Role**. The role appears in the list as a **Custom role**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Restrict a role to specific resources

By default, a custom role reaches no resource through scopes: its holders reach only the agents and knowledge bases they own or that are shared with them. To let the role reach resources without sharing each one:

1. Open the role, then find the resource under **Platform Permissions**, such as **Agents** or **Storage / Knowledge**.
2. In the scope section of that resource, do one of the following:
   - To reach every resource of that type, check **All resources**.
   - To reach specific resources, enter the ID of each resource in **Resource ID...**.
3. Select **Save**.

A role whose scopes name specific resources, without **All resources**, cannot create resources of that type.

## Assign the role to a member

1. In Governance, select **Members**.
2. In the member's row, open the actions menu and select **Change Role**.
3. Select the role, then select **Save**.

The new role applies once the member reloads the page. {/* REVIEW: confirm whether a role change applies on the next request or only after the session token is refreshed. */} To assign a role to people before they join, set it on an [email invite](/products/ai-governance/identity-access#inviting-members), an [invitation code](/products/ai-governance/identity-access#invitation-codes), or a [join rule](/products/ai-governance/identity-access#join-rules).

## Verify the result

1. Ask a member who holds the role to switch to this organization and reload the page.
2. Confirm that the products the role allows appear in their menu, and that the others do not.
3. Ask them to open a resource the role scopes or that is shared with them, and to try an action the role does not allow. The action is refused with `Access denied: missing permission '<product>:<resource>:<action>'`.

## Edit a system role

Every system role except **Owner** can be edited: open it, change its permissions, and select **Save**. The edited version is stored for this organization and replaces the system definition from then on, including when a later release adds permissions to the system role. Check the system roles you have edited after each upgrade.

## Undo the change

- To restore a member's previous access, assign their previous role with **Change Role**.
- To remove a custom role, first assign another role to every member who holds it, then select the delete action on the role and confirm with **Delete**. Members who still hold a deleted role need a new role before they regain access.
