title: 'Dictate a message'
description: 'End user: speak a message instead of typing it in the chat input, review the transcribed text, then send it.'

Use dictation when speaking is faster than typing. Select the microphone button in the chat input, speak, and select it again: your words appear as text in the input, ready for you to review and send. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

## Before you begin

- **Who:** any user of the web chat.
- **Where:** the chat input of **Chat** (home screen and conversations), the Agent Factory playground, the [embedded chat widget](/products/agent-factory/embed#features) when its owner enables dictation, and the **Ask** bar of a [Collection](/products/ai-collection/data-management#asking-questions).
- **Requires:** a microphone, and a browser that can record audio. Whether you see the microphone button depends on your browser, on your organization's transcription model and on your role:

| Your situation | Desktop Chrome or Edge | Firefox, Safari, Brave, mobile browsers |
|---|---|---|
| A transcription model is available to you | Dictation through your organization's model | Dictation through your organization's model |
| Your organization has no transcription model, or its allowed models leave it out | Dictation through the browser's own speech service, unless your administrators turned this fallback off | No microphone button |
| Your organization has a transcription model, but your role does not grant **Use Audio Transcriptions** (this includes visitors not signed in to an embedded chat widget) | No microphone button | No microphone button |

See [How dictation works](#how-dictation-works) for what each mode means for your audio. If the microphone button is missing, see [The microphone button is missing](#the-microphone-button-is-missing).

## Dictate a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    In the chat input, select the microphone button (**Dictate**). The first time, your browser asks for permission to use the microphone: allow it.

    While the browser prompt is open, the button shows **Waiting for microphone access…**. Select it again to cancel the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak">
    Say your message. You can record for up to 2 minutes: at 2 minutes, recording stops on its own and what you said is turned into text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop recording">
    Select the microphone button again. The button shows a spinner and **Transcribing…** until the text is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    The transcribed text is added to the input, after any text you had already typed. Correct it if needed, then send it as you would any typed message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the result

1. Type a few words in the chat input, then dictate a short sentence.
2. Confirm that the sentence appears after your typed words and that no message was sent.
3. Send the message and confirm that the agent answers it like any typed message.

## How dictation works

Dictation runs in one of two modes. When a transcription model is available to you, the chat always uses it, whatever your browser.

### With a transcription model

Your browser records the audio and sends it to the Prisme.ai platform, where your organization's transcription model turns it into text. Dictation works the same way in every modern browser and behind corporate proxies.

- **Your audio is never stored.** It is processed in memory and discarded once transcribed. It is not saved in storage, in the activity feed, or in logs. The transcribed text is then handled exactly like text you type.
- **Language.** The chat sends your interface language to the transcription model as a hint, so that short dictations such as "Yes" or "Oui" are recognized correctly. In the embedded chat widget, the model detects the language on its own.

### Browser fallback (desktop Chrome and Edge)

When your organization has no transcription model, or its allowed models leave it out, desktop Chrome and Edge dictate with the browser's own speech service, unless your administrators turned this fallback off. The browser sends your audio directly to its vendor's speech service, Google for Chrome and Microsoft for Edge, and never to Prisme.ai.

- **No guarantee on your audio.** Prisme.ai never receives this audio, and cannot guarantee whether or how long Google or Microsoft retain it. Your organization's model settings, data residency and analytics do not apply to it.
- **Language.** The browser listens in its own language, not in your interface language.
- **Network.** It may not work behind a corporate proxy, or on computers whose policies turn off online speech recognition.
- **When it is not used.** The chat never falls back to the browser when your organization has a transcription model but your role does not grant **Use Audio Transcriptions**: your administrator turned dictation off for you. Visitors who are not signed in to an embedded chat widget never hold this permission, so they only get the fallback when the organization has no transcription model. The chat does not fall back either while it is still checking whether a transcription model is available to you, or when that check fails: you then see no microphone button.

### Mobile apps

The Prisme.ai mobile apps are not affected: they keep using your device's speech recognition.

## For administrators

Dictation relies on a transcription model in the LLM Gateway model catalog. A user dictates through your transcription model when all of these are true:

- the platform or the organization has a transcription model, and the organization's allowed models include it when the organization restricts models;
- the user's role grants the `llm-gateway:models:audio-transcriptions` permission (**Use Audio Transcriptions**), which the system **Member**, **Agent Maker** and **Builder** roles include by default. A custom role must have it added.

Without the permission, a user of an organization that has a transcription model cannot dictate at all, in any browser: removing **Use Audio Transcriptions** from a role turns dictation off for its holders.

When the organization has no transcription model, or its allowed models leave it out, desktop Chrome and Edge fall back to the browser's own speech service, which sends the audio to Google or Microsoft. The permission is checked as soon as a transcription model exists, even one the allowed models leave out: a role without **Use Audio Transcriptions** then gets no fallback either. Only when there is no transcription model at all is the permission not checked. Leaving transcription models out of the allowed models therefore does not turn dictation off in those browsers for roles that hold the permission. Two settings control the fallback, both on by default:

| Setting | Where | Effect when off |
|---|---|---|
| **Browser dictation fallback** | Govern › Models › Providers › **Defaults** (platform, `defaults.browser_dictation`) | No user of the platform dictates through a browser speech service |
| **Allow browser dictation fallback** | Organization › Agents Controls › Models (organization, `allow_browser_dictation`) | No member of the organization dictates through a browser speech service |


With either setting off, users without a transcription model see no microphone button. An organization can only turn the fallback off: it cannot turn it back on when the platform turned it off. To keep dictated audio within your organization's providers and still offer dictation, make a transcription model available to every user who dictates.

The chat checks what is available to a user once, when it loads. A model, a permission or a setting you change applies to a user after they reload the page.

See [Transcription model for dictation](/products/ai-governance/model-governance#transcription-model-for-dictation) to set it up, and the [`POST /v1/audio/transcriptions` reference](/llm-gateway/audio/transcribe-audio-into-text) to call the same transcription service from your own code.

## Troubleshooting

### The microphone button is missing

**Cause:** one of these applies:

- your browser cannot record audio, for example an old browser or a page embedded without microphone access;
- your organization has a transcription model, but your role does not grant the `llm-gateway:models:audio-transcriptions` permission (**Use Audio Transcriptions**), or you are not signed in to an embedded chat widget: dictation is off for you in every browser;
- your organization has no transcription model, or its allowed models do not include one, and either your browser is not desktop Chrome or Edge, or your administrators turned the browser dictation fallback off;
- the chat is still checking whether a transcription model is available, or could not reach the LLM Gateway: the button appears once the check succeeds, after a page reload in the second case;
- your administrator just changed the model, your role or the fallback settings: the chat checks them only when it loads.

**Owner:** you for the browser, your organization administrator otherwise.

**Resolution:** reload the page. Try an up-to-date desktop Chrome or Edge, which dictate even without a transcription model unless the fallback is turned off. If the button is still missing, ask your administrator to check the [transcription model setup](/products/ai-governance/model-governance#transcription-model-for-dictation) and, if you have a custom role, to add **Use Audio Transcriptions** to it.

### Microphone access denied. Allow microphone in your browser settings.

**Cause:** the browser blocked the microphone for this site, usually because the permission prompt was refused.

**Owner:** you.

**Resolution:** open the site permissions in your browser (the icon on the left of the address bar), allow the microphone for the Prisme.ai site, reload the page, and dictate again.

### Microphone not available. Check your audio device.

**Cause:** the browser found no usable microphone, or another application holds it.

**Owner:** you.

**Resolution:** connect or select a microphone in your system sound settings, close applications that use it, and try again.

### No speech detected. Try again.

**Cause:** the recording contained no speech, for example when the microphone was muted or you stopped recording right away.

**Owner:** you.

**Resolution:** check that your microphone is not muted, then start recording and speak before you stop it.

### Your browser's speech service can't be reached. Ask your administrator to set up a transcription model.

**Cause:** no transcription model is available to you, so Chrome or Edge dictates with its own speech service, and the browser cannot reach it. Common reasons: a proxy or firewall filters the vendor's speech service, a policy on a managed computer turns it off (for example **Online speech recognition** in Windows privacy settings), or the vendor's service is temporarily down.

**Owner:** your organization administrator.

**Resolution:** ask your administrator to make a [transcription model](/products/ai-governance/model-governance#transcription-model-for-dictation) available to you: dictation then goes through the Prisme.ai platform and no longer depends on the browser's service. Meanwhile, another browser or another network may work.

### Transcription failed. Please try again.

**Cause:** the transcription service could not process the recording, for example because of a temporary provider error.

**Owner:** you; your administrator if the error persists.

**Resolution:** dictate again. If every attempt fails, your administrator can run the **Test** button of the transcription model in Governance.

### Recording too long to transcribe. Try a shorter dictation.

**Cause:** the recording is larger than the transcription service accepts.

**Owner:** you.

**Resolution:** dictate your message in shorter parts. Each dictation is added after the text already in the input.
