Building great agents is only half the job, people have to **know about them, understand them, and come back to them**. Engage is the product that closes that loop. Found under the **Run** menu, it's where administrators **communicate with users and animate adoption** across the platform: announce new agents and features, share updates, and keep your community active on the foundation you've built.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/engage-dashboard.png" alt="Engage dashboard with announcement stats, recent announcements, and quick actions" />
</Frame>

Where [Insights](../ai-insights/overview) tells you *how* adoption is going, Engage is how you *drive* it.

## Where Engage fits in the story

The foundation gives you a full lifecycle, and Engage is the step most platforms forget:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build">
    Create agents and applications with [Agent Creator](../agent-factory/overview) and [Builder](../ai-builder/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consume">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure">
    Track quality, usage, and compliance with [Insights](../ai-insights/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage">
    Announce, inform, and re-engage users to turn a catalog of agents into real, sustained adoption.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

Engage is organized into three areas.

### Dashboard

Your communications at a glance: how many announcements are **Total**, **Active**, and in **Drafts**, a list of recent announcements, and quick actions to create a new one or review existing ones.

### Announcements

The heart of Engage. Each announcement carries:

- **Title & summary**, the summary is what users see first, in cards and notifications
- **Rich content**, a formatted body (bold, italic, underline, headings, bullet and numbered lists) for the full message
- **Icon & link**, an optional emoji and a click-through link (an internal route like `/features/new` or an external URL)
- **Expiration**, an optional date after which the announcement stops showing
- **Status**, save as a **Draft** to keep working, or **Publish** to make it live

  <img src="/images/engage-create-announcement.png" alt="Create Announcement form with language tabs, title, summary, and rich-text content editor" />

### All Notifications

The user-facing side of Engage. Published announcements become **notifications** users can filter (all / unread / read), open to read in full, and dismiss once handled.

## Targeting: the right message to the right people

Engage inherits the platform's governance model, so **who sees an announcement is defined with the same roles and permissions as everything else**. For each announcement you choose the audience across four dimensions (combined so a user must match the targeting to receive it):

| Dimension | Example |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Everyone, or only users of Agent Creator, Chat, Builder… |
| **Roles** | Everyone, or only Owners / Admins / Editors / Viewers (or your custom org roles) |
| **Permissions** | Restrict to holders of a specific permission |
| **Users** | Send to specific individuals |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/engage-announcement-targeting.png" alt="Announcement targeting by product and role, with a super-admin option to target a specific organization" />
</Frame>

<Note>
**Central + per-org, as everywhere on the platform.** Organization admins publish to their own organization. Platform super-admins can additionally target a **specific organization** by slug, the same two-level governance model that runs through [Governe](../ai-governance/overview).
</Note>
