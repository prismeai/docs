Adoption at scale needs a clear operating model: who runs the platform, who sets policy, who validates, who builds, and who uses. You define it; the platform makes each right enforceable through [roles and permissions](/products/ai-governance/identity-access).

## The squads

Four squads keep the platform alive and industrialize use cases, backed by solution architects.

| Squad | Mission |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Run the platform and release management: operations, supervision, upgrades. |
| **Animation & Self-care** | Drive adoption and share reusable assets: skills, tools, MCP, and agent templates. |
| **Use-cases (Onboarding)** | Frame and onboard complex use cases with the business teams. |
| **Architecture & Recommendations** | Standards and best practices, reuse and mutualization, regulatory framing, and LLM choice (the platform stays vendor-neutral). |

## Driving adoption: the Animation squad

Adoption does not happen on its own. The Animation squad keeps the community active with a few concrete, recurring rituals:

- **Use-case webinars**: teams present what they built, and others reuse it. Sharing beats rebuilding.
- **Agent bar**: a recurring clinic where anyone brings an idea and leaves with a working agent, with help on hand.
- **Agents on demand**: simple agents built live in a few minutes, to show how low the barrier is.
- **Shared assets**: skills, tools, MCP servers, and agent templates published for everyone to reuse.

## The rights, level by level

Governance runs on levels, and **each level can only tighten the one above**. Sharing and publishing rights, and guardrails, are granted centrally, not self-assigned.

- **Platform Squad**: runs and releases; sets platform guardrails and can force [hooks](/products/agent-factory/hooks) (for example data classification controls).
- **Architecture / Org admin**: sets org policy and standards, [model access](/products/ai-governance/model-governance), and org-level guardrails.
- **Committees** (compliance and security): validate the compliance criteria of an agent, approve the guardrails to activate, and grant sharing rights.
- **AI Champions**: build agents and, once validated, share them to a group or the organization.
- **Everyone**: uses agents and creates agents for themselves. By default, no one shares or publishes their own agent.

For the underlying roles, permissions, and service accounts, see [Identity & Access](/products/ai-governance/identity-access). For how the levels bound each other, see [Opening strategies](./opening-strategies).
