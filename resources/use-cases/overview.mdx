Use cases, not features. Whatever the business problem, it takes one of **three shapes** on the platform. Start from the shape that fits, then combine them. Most enterprises start on **SaaS** for speed and move to **self-hosted** when criticality or sovereignty require it.

## Agents

Talk to it, it acts. A conversational agent that reasons over your knowledge, calls tools, and acts on your systems.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/use-case-agents.svg" alt="Agents: people ask through Chat, Store, Teams, or a website embed; the agent (LLM routed across providers, skills via tools and MCP, knowledge via RAG and memory) reads and writes to your systems (CRM, ITSM, ERP, documents, APIs) via connectors and MCP; governed and traceable, with answers sourced and human approval on sensitive actions." />
</Frame>

- **Employees** — secure, multi-LLM assistant for daily work
- **HR** — cited FAQ on leave, payroll, and mobility
- **Legal** — conversational research over internal case law, contract Q&A
- **Support** — agent assist with real-time, cited answer suggestions
- **Sales** — prospecting brief: the agent gathers data across CRM, news, and your systems, and prepares the meeting and the pitch
- **Data** — conversational analyst that queries your warehouses and produces the analysis

## Agentic workflows

Headless automation. A process that runs on its own (endpoint, event, or schedule), calls agents where judgment is needed, and acts on your systems. No UI required.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/use-case-workflow.svg" alt="Agentic workflows: triggered by an endpoint, an event, or a schedule; the workflow runs steps and conditions, calls agents where judgment helps, and asks for human approval on sensitive steps; it updates CRM, ERP, and ticketing, generates reports, and notifies; governed and traceable end to end by correlation ID." />
</Frame>

- **Finance** — continuous close, reconciliation, audit-ready regulatory reporting
- **Sales** — post-meeting update: draft the CRM notes, update SAP, and refresh the Power BI dashboard
- **Procurement** — procure-to-pay: invoice matching and approvals
- **IT** — autonomous service desk resolution and AIOps remediation
- **Compliance** — automated assembly of AI Act and DORA dossiers
- **Security** — SIEM/EDR alert triage and orchestrated incident response
- **Operations** — document processing: extract and route contracts, invoices, claims

## Agentic portals (full-stack)

The highest-value shape: a React front-end, agents, and workflows in one custom portal that replaces a patchwork of siloed tools, connected to your systems and governed.

- **Sales cockpit** (the brief and the post-meeting updates in one place) · Ephemeral event or crisis apps

## One need, three shapes

The same business need can take any of the three shapes. A **Sales assistant** can be an **agent** (prepare the prospecting brief), an **agentic workflow** (update CRM, SAP, and Power BI after each meeting), or a **full portal** (a sales cockpit that does both, with a UI). You pick the shape per use case, on the same governed foundation.

## Generate it with a prompt

You do not have to build these by hand. Connect the **Prisme.ai [MCP](/products/ai-securechat/mcp-connections)** to VS Code and drive it with the coding agent your team already uses, **Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, or Codex**. Paste a prompt like these and let it scaffold the workspace, then review and version in Git.

<CodeGroup>

```text Agent
Using the Prisme.ai MCP, create an agent in the "sales" workspace that prepares a
prospecting brief: read the account from our CRM connector, search recent news,
and draft a one-page brief and a suggested pitch. Ground it on our sales-playbook
knowledge base. Require human approval before it writes anything back.
```

```text Agentic workflow
Using the Prisme.ai MCP, add an automation to the "sales" workspace triggered by a
webhook after a meeting: take the meeting notes, draft the CRM update, push the
opportunity fields to SAP, and refresh the Power BI dataset. Log every step and
ask for approval before the SAP write.
```

```text Full-stack portal
Using the Prisme.ai MCP and the starter SPA (github.com/prismeai/starter-spa),
scaffold a sales cockpit: a React front-end with an account list and a brief view,
wired to the prospecting agent and the post-meeting workflow above, plus connectors
to CRM, SAP, and Power BI. Version it in Git.
```

</CodeGroup>

## By sector

Regulated industries most often deploy self-hosted, for data residency and sovereignty.

| Sector | Flagship use case | Typical deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **Defense** | Multi-source intelligence fusion (OSINT/SIGINT/IMINT), briefs for validation | Self-hosted / air-gapped |
| **Aerospace** | Autonomous AOG resolution: parts sourcing, airworthiness check, logistics plan | Self-hosted / private |
| **Banking & Insurance** | Continuous financial close, reconciliation, audit-ready reporting | Self-hosted / sovereign |
| **Energy** | Autonomous grid outage triage and balancing (AMI/OMS/SCADA) | Sovereign / self-hosted |
| **Public Sector** | End-to-end citizen request management: classify, route, track | Sovereign private cloud / self-hosted |
| **Manufacturing** | Agentic supply chain: inventory, suppliers, logistics | Self-hosted / edge |
| **Telecom** | Network supervision, customer resolution, field orchestration | Your control |
