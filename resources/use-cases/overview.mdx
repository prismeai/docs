Use cases, not features. Each starts from a business problem and runs on the same governed foundation. Most enterprises start on **SaaS** for speed, then move to **self-hosted** when criticality or sovereignty require it.

## By business function

### Finance
Agents that analyze, reconcile, and report autonomously, without exposing sensitive data.
- Credit memo drafting: gather financials and rules, draft the memo and covenants for approval
- Continuous financial close: real-time monitoring, anomaly detection, pre-approved corrective entries
- Reconciliation and break investigation across accounts, ledgers, and settlement systems
- Automated, audit-ready regulatory reporting (multi-system collection)

Products: Agent Creator · Knowledges · Builder · Governe

### Human Resources
HR agents that answer, orchestrate onboarding, and process documents, with privacy by design.
- Onboarding assistant orchestrating accounts, access, and training for each new hire
- Cited conversational HR FAQ (leave, payroll, mobility)
- Document processing: extraction and filing of HR records

Products: Chat · Knowledges · Agent Creator · Builder

### Legal
Agents that analyze contracts and case law, extract obligations and risks, and draft, grounded in your own corpora.
- Contract review and comparison with obligation and risk extraction
- Conversational legal research over internal case law
- Automated contract management: summary, redlining, deadline alerts

Products: Knowledges · Agent Creator · Chat · Governe

### Compliance
Supervisory agents that monitor AI usage, gather evidence, and produce reporting, AI Act compliant.
- Continuous mapping and control of the organization's AI usage
- Regulatory watch: map changes to your internal policies
- Automated assembly of compliance dossiers (AI Act, DORA)

Products: Governe · Insights · Agent Creator · Builder

### Customer Support
Agents that resolve end-to-end, access CRM and ticketing, and escalate with full context.
- Autonomous case resolution across CRM, ERP, and support
- Agent assist: real-time cited answer suggestions
- Automatic routing and qualification of incoming requests

Products: Agent Creator · Knowledges · Builder · Insights

## By role

### CISO / Security
An agentic SOC, sovereign data, and end-to-end observability.
- Autonomous SIEM/EDR alert triage with false-positive filtering
- Orchestrated incident response: containment proposed for human approval
- Continuous threat hunting and CTI across logs
- Posture governance and audit evidence (NIS2, AI Act)

Products: Governe · LLM Gateway · Insights · Agent Creator

### CIO / IT
Industrialize AI as a capability of your IT: one unified, governed, sovereign orchestration layer.
- Autonomous service desk: end-to-end IT ticket resolution
- AIOps and remediation: root-cause analysis and policy-based routine fixes
- Cross-function multi-agent orchestration (IT, HR, Finance)
- Legacy modernization: code generation and testing

Products: Builder · Governe · Agent Creator · LLM Gateway

### CDO / Data
From data to insight in minutes, with governed agents that query, join, and harden your data.
- Conversational data analyst: query warehouses and produce analysis
- Automated cataloging and quality (profiling, anomalies, remediation)
- Governance of agent data access, logged for audit
- Automated lineage and GDPR compliance

Products: Knowledges · Builder · Governe · Insights

### Business Leaders
Accelerate your processes without growing headcount: agents that run business cycles end-to-end.
- Procure-to-pay automation (invoice matching, approvals)
- Autonomous customer-case resolution across CRM/ERP/support
- Automated contract management (summary, risks, obligations)
- Operations steering and forecasting with corrective actions

Products: Agent Creator · Builder · Insights · Chat

## By sector

Regulated industries most often deploy self-hosted, for data residency and sovereignty.

| Sector | Flagship use case | Typical deployment |
|---|---|---|
| **Defense** | Multi-source intelligence fusion (OSINT/SIGINT/IMINT), briefs for validation | Self-hosted / air-gapped |
| **Aerospace** | Autonomous AOG resolution: parts sourcing, airworthiness check, logistics plan | Self-hosted / private |
| **Banking & Insurance** | Continuous financial close, reconciliation, audit-ready reporting | Self-hosted / sovereign |
| **Energy** | Autonomous grid outage triage and balancing (AMI/OMS/SCADA) | Sovereign / self-hosted |
| **Public Sector** | End-to-end citizen request management: classify, route, track | Sovereign private cloud / self-hosted |
| **Manufacturing** | Agentic supply chain: inventory, suppliers, logistics | Self-hosted / edge |
| **Telecom** | Network supervision, customer resolution, field orchestration | Your control |
