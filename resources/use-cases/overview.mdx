Use cases, not features. Each one starts from a business problem and is delivered on the same governed foundation. Most enterprises start on **SaaS** for speed, then move to **self-hosted** when criticality or sovereignty require it.

## By business outcome

| Use case | Products | Maturity | The gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Internal knowledge assistant** (HR/IT self-service on your procedures) | Chat · Knowledges · Agent Creator | Adopt to Specialize | Fewer tickets, sourced answers |
| **Customer support augmentation** (triage, answer, escalate) | Agent Creator · Knowledges · Collections | Specialize | Faster handling, better quality |
| **Document processing** (contracts, invoices, claims: extract and route) | Agent Creator · Builder · Collections | Specialize to Scale | Automation with an audit trail |
| **Compliance and regulatory reporting** (watch, control, GDPR/AI Act) | Governe · Insights · Agent Creator | Scale | Traceability and audit proof |
| **Custom agentic portals** (a React front-end, agents, and workflows that replace scattered internal tools) | Builder · connectors · Governe | Scale and transform | Lower software costs, one stack |
| **Agents in your channels** (Teams, ServiceNow, your website) | Chat · connectors and MCP · Builder | Adopt to Scale | Adoption where people work |
| **Multi-agent process orchestration** (procurement, onboarding) | Agent Creator (orchestrator) · Builder | Scale and transform | Governed end to end |

## Flagship theme: custom agentic portals

The highest-value pattern for enterprises: replace a patchwork of siloed internal tools with **one custom portal** built on the platform, agents and workflows behind a React front-end, connected to your systems, and governed.
